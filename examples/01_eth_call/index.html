Full EVM execution. In your browser.

A WebAssembly EVM runs locally in this tab, synced to Avalanche C-Chain state via WebSocket. When a new block arrives, your browser executes eth_call against its own copy of the chain — no RPC round-trip, no waiting.

The table below runs the same call two ways: locally in WASM (triggered on each block) and via the Avalanche public RPC (polling sequentially). Watch the local column fill in first.
